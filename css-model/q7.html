Typography in Practice

Good typography is felt more than it is noticed. It is what makes a page comfortable to read for minutes at a time without any conscious effort.

This paragraph uses a serif font family at a slightly larger size, with generous line-height and letter-spacing to keep long lines from feeling cramped. Notice how bold text and italic text are styled with distinct colors rather than the browser default.

This second paragraph switches to a sans-serif family with tighter line-height, a smaller size, and center alignment, to contrast against the paragraph above it and show how typographic choices change the feel of the same content.

The first letter of this paragraph is enlarged using the ::first-letter pseudo-element, and the first line is styled in full capitals using ::first-line, a classic technique borrowed from print typography to draw the reader's eye in.
